About this project

oriz-qr

A free, 100% client-side QR studio: generate (URL / Wi-Fi / vCard / UPI & more), customize, and scan — all in your browser.

What it is

oriz-qr generates QR codes for seven payload types and scans QR codes back — entirely on your device. Build a code, restyle it, add a logo, export PNG or SVG; or switch to Scan to read a code with your camera or from an image.

Key features

  • 7 payload types — URL, text, Wi-Fi, contact (vCard 3.0), UPI payment, email, SMS.
  • Customize — any ink + paper color, center logo overlay, error-correction L/M/Q/H.
  • Export — download PNG (raster) or SVG (vector).
  • Scan — live camera decode or drop/pick a QR image; open-link + copy actions.
  • AI describe (optional) — type plain language and it picks the type + fills fields.

Tech

  • Client-only Astro + React 19 islands + Tailwind v4 — static, no backend.
  • g4f AI via @chirag127/oz-ai (keyless, multi-provider failover; optional).
  • PWA-installable, offline-capable.
  • 100% in-browser — no upload, no signup, no tracking.
